# 素材生成指令（桌面植物挂件）

> 用途：补齐/重做游戏插画素材。生成后按本文路径存放，我来抠图、压缩、接入。
> 尺寸：正方形 1:1，1024×1024 以上（越大越好）。格式 JPG 或 PNG 均可。

---

## 一、怎么拼 prompt

每条 prompt = **风格前缀** + **主体描述** + **通用后缀**。

### 风格前缀（每条都加）

```text
Cozy healing watercolor hand-drawn illustration, warm cream paper background
(#FBF6EC), bold closed black ink outlines around EVERY shape, rich saturated
watercolor fills, volume created ONLY by watercolor density gradient (lighter
pigment on the upper-left, deeper pigment on the lower-right; NO glossy white
highlights, NO reflections, no plastic shine), fixed front view, centered
composition, at least 8% empty margin on all sides, no text, no watermark,
no border, no shadow, perfectly flat background with no paper texture.
```

### 通用后缀

- **植株类**（plants）：
```text
Single plant growing from a small dark-brown soil mound at the bottom center,
no pot, whole plant fully visible, nothing touching the image edges,
background completely flat solid cream with no texture and no vignette.
```
- **花盆 / 果实 / 料理**：
```text
No plant, no soil, nothing touching the image edges, background completely
flat solid cream with no texture and no vignette.
```

### 完整示例（草莓成熟）

```text
Cozy healing watercolor hand-drawn illustration, warm cream paper background
(#FBF6EC), bold closed black ink outlines around EVERY shape, rich saturated
watercolor fills, volume created ONLY by watercolor density gradient (lighter
pigment on the upper-left, deeper pigment on the lower-right; NO glossy white
highlights, NO reflections, no plastic shine), fixed front view, centered
composition, at least 8% empty margin on all sides, no text, no watermark,
no border, no shadow, perfectly flat background with no paper texture.

A compact low strawberry plant with several plump ripe red strawberries,
tiny yellow seeds, fresh green leafy calyxes and bright green serrated
trifoliate leaves; each berry and each leaf has smooth watercolor
light-to-dark gradient; a few dark-brown soil crumbs scattered beside
the soil mound.

Single plant growing from a small dark-brown soil mound at the bottom center,
no pot, whole plant fully visible, nothing touching the image edges,
background completely flat solid cream with no texture and no vignette.
```

### 三条硬性要求（决定抠图干不干净）

1. 背景**纯色、无纸纹、无渐变、无投影**，主体四周留 ≥8% 空白；
2. 白花、浅黄/黄绿果实、嫩叶等浅色区域必须被**闭合的深色描边**包住，不能与背景直接相连；
3. 主体不接触画面边缘；植株类要带完整土堆。

## 六、交付方式

1. 按上表路径存放即可（`.jpg`）；也可以整批丢进 `/workspace/.uploads`，告诉我文件名对应关系。
2. 我会：去背景（抠成透明 webp）→ 裁掉透明留白 → 压缩 → 接入 `js/data.js` → 逐张目检。
3. 优先级：**柠檬 4 张 + 蓝莓 6 张 > 3 个花盆 + 5 个果实 > 17 道料理**。
4. 每个阶段尽量保持同一株植物形态一致：树干粗细、叶片形状、构图位置不要跳变（唯一允许的变化是生长与结果）。
